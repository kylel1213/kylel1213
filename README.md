# Kyle Lensch

Audio engineer and systems manager. Austin, TX, with NYC roots.

Twelve years across festival main stages, electronic music venues, touring, and
high-profile private events. Most recently ran audio across Avant Gardner's three
Brooklyn venues: Brooklyn Mirage, Great Hall, and Kings Hall.

FOH and monitor engineering, systems design and operation, wireless RF
coordination, backline support, custom systems fabrication. Dante Level 2
certified.

### Currently building

- **[SampleShot](https://sampleshot.studio)** — sampling and production app for
  iPhone and Mac. Lossless capture, on-device stem separation, acoustic
  similarity matching, DAW export.
- **[Rekursive](https://rekursive.music)** — a production practice built on
  routing source material through generative models and back into the DAW, with
  a human deciding what survives each pass.
- **[Monky](https://sleepmonky.com)** — a plush comfort companion that turns
  calming audio frequencies into vibration you feel rather than hear.

Most application repos here are private while in development. The public ones are
support and legal pages for shipped apps.
